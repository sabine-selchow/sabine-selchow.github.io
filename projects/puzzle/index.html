Word Puzzle

Choose your setting. Enter your search words. Press 'Generate the Puzzle'. Press 'Print the Puzzle'.

When you have generated your puzzle you can hover over the search words to see where the words are hidden in the puzzle.

Your entries will reset when you leave the page (or when you press 'Clear All').
